# Winning Strategy: Micro-Merchant Platform vs Competitors

Sep 30, 2026 · @CHETRA

## Strategy in brief

Win by owning the step after payment: delivery. Vendra and Angkoro already sell the same core (shop link, KHQR, COD, order dashboard) for $10–15/month, so matching them is table stakes, not a reason to switch.

- **Match** their core modules at launch, with no product limits, at a lower entry price.
- **Beat** them on delivery (quote, book and track a driver from the order) and on payment proof (automatic KHQR verification, no screenshots).
- **Remove switching pain**: free import of their catalog, free setup, and the first month free for sellers who show a competitor invoice.
- **Lock in** with things that grow in value: customer list, repeat-buyer tools and sales history that stay with us.

## Where competitors are weak

The biggest shared gap is that delivery stops at settings: nobody shows booking a real driver from an order.

| Weakness | Who | Why it matters to sellers | How we exploit it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery is settings only (fees, zones, pickup), no booking or live tracking | Vendra, Angkoro | Sellers still message drivers by hand for every order | One-tap "send to driver" with quote and tracking link |
| Telegram-first; Facebook sellers are secondary | Vendra, Angkoro | Most Cambodian buyers discover products on Facebook | Web shop link that works best from Facebook and TikTok too |
| Price rises with staff and product count | Angkoro | A 2-person shop pays $29/month | Staff seats included in the main plan |
| Few operations tools: no inventory, discounts or analytics described | Vendra | Growing shops outgrow it | Include these in the main plan |
| Weak trust signals: reused "TeleStore" testimonials | Angkoro | Sellers doubt claims | Real named shops, visible reviews, Khmer video demos |
| English-heavy site and sign-up | Vendra | Low-tech sellers drop off | Khmer-first sign-up with voice or video help |
| Payment tool only, no shop | KHQRPay | Seller still needs a storefront | Bundle verified KHQR inside the shop |
| No payment or delivery at all | Khmer Mart | Deals still happen in chat | Full checkout, then delivery |

## Switching plan

Make switching cost the seller nothing: no money, no data entry, no lost orders.

1. **Find them.** Search Facebook and Telegram for shop links on vendra and angkoro domains, and join Cambodian seller groups.
2. **Offer a switch deal.** Show a competitor invoice, get the months left on it free (up to 3), plus free setup.
3. **Move their shop for them.** We import products, photos and prices within 24 hours; the seller only checks and approves.
4. **Run both side by side.** Seller keeps the old shop for 1–2 weeks while testing ours, so no orders are lost.
5. **Prove the win in week one.** Book their first deliveries through us and show time saved and verified payments.
6. **Keep them.** Monthly Telegram report of sales and repeat buyers; a referral reward of 1 free month per seller they bring.

Message to sellers, in one line: "Same price or less, plus we send your driver for you."
